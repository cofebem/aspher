# Ns=16384 rebaseline against the corrected solver (2026-09-09)

First large-grid measurement after the D1/D2 correctness and accuracy work
(A01–A07, A09). The promotion rule requires rebaselining against the corrected
solver before any D3 variant is judged, and the existing Ns=16384 numbers in
`CLAUDE.md` all predate it.

Ledger: `data/bench_ledger.jsonl`. Log: `data/bench_16384.log`.

## Conditions

Revision `982ef0c`, clean tree, pocketfft, g++ 11.4.0 `-O3 -DNDEBUG`,
i7-13700H, 20 cores, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=20`, `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`, 24.6 GiB
available, machine otherwise idle. Workload `rough-H0.8`: rfgen self-affine,
H=0.8, rms=0.02, k_low=12/Ns, k_high=0.33, seed 42, p̄=0.002, surface
`39149fed6cfa3bbe216a0a0994516151`. `coarsest=64`, `q=4`, `leaf_side=16`,
`tol=1e-8`, `light_result=True`.

**Uncertainty limitation:** one repetition per case (the plan permits this for
expensive jobs with the limitation stated). No confidence intervals; treat
ratios as indicative and single-digit percentages as noise.

## Results

| variant | wall | peak RSS | it | fw_error | penetration | area | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `h2-f32-active` | 124.7 s | 9.12 GiB | 47 | 7.52e-07 | 7.93e-07 | 4.45e-4 | converged |
| `h2-f64-active` | 314.6 s | 9.31 GiB | 72 | 1.49e-10 | 1.43e-10 | 4.45e-4 | converged |
| `h2-f32` (standard) | 647.0 s | 15.99 GiB | 43 | 1.57e-06 | — | 4.45e-4 | converged |
| `h2-f64` (standard) | — | — | — | — | — | — | **refused by preflight** |

All three completed runs agree on contact area to every printed digit across
two precisions *and* two algorithms — the cross-check that matters most.

### Phase attribution — the main finding

| variant | coarse levels | finest preconditioner | finest matvec |
|---|---|---|---|
| `h2-f32-active` | 62.7 s (50%) | 51.5 s (41%) | **5.2 s (4%)** |
| `h2-f64-active` | 140.0 s (45%) | 149.4 s (47%) | **19.5 s (6%)** |
| `h2-f32` standard | 70.8 s (11%) | 48.9 s (8%) | **501.3 s (78%)** |

On the standard path the matvec is 78% of the run. The active-set solver
removes essentially all of it — 501.3 s → 5.2 s, a factor of 96 on that
component — and what is left is two costs it does not touch:

* the **|q| Fourier preconditioner**, which still runs on the full Ns×Ns grid
  every iteration and is *unchanged* between the two paths (48.9 s standard vs
  51.5 s active), and
* the **coarse cascade**, 64→128→…→8192, which costs about as much as the
  finest level.

So on the configuration anyone should actually run at this size, the H² matvec
is 4–6% of wall time.

### Active vs standard, float, same session

5.19× faster (647.0 → 124.7 s) and 1.75× less memory (15.99 → 9.12 GiB).
Both converged, both at effective tolerance 2e-6, identical contact area.

### Single precision buys time, not memory, on the active path

`h2-f32-active` 9.12 GiB vs `h2-f64-active` 9.31 GiB — a 2% difference. The
compressed O(N_c) CG state was never the peak; what dominates is the full-grid
**double** arrays present in both configurations (caller gap 2 GiB, prolonged
warm start 2 GiB, returned pressure 2 GiB) plus the surface-generation
transient. Float still buys 2.5× in *time* (124.7 vs 314.6 s), which is the
honest reason to choose it.

`CLAUDE.md`'s standing advice that `single_precision` is the memory lever at
Ns=16384 was measured on the **standard** path (15.99 GiB float there, and the
double standard path does not fit at all) and does not transfer to the active
one.

### The `h2-f64` refusal

Predicted 21.82 GiB; with the 1.25 headroom that is 27.3 GiB against 24.5 GiB
available, so the preflight refused it and recorded
`run_status="skipped_preflight"` with the full estimate rather than letting it
run for ten minutes and be OOM-killed.

This is a *marginal* refusal and should be read as such: the 2026-07-10 record
has standard double completing at Ns=16384 with a measured peak of 24.30 GiB.
The model predicts 21.82 GiB — about 10% under that measurement, consistent
with its other errors — and the headroom then turns a case that historically
just fitted into a refusal. Correct behaviour for an unattended job on a
machine in use; a deliberate attempt should pass `--headroom 1.05`.

### Preflight accuracy

| variant | predicted | measured | error |
|---|---|---|---|
| `h2-f32-active` | 8.29 GiB | 9.12 GiB | −9% |
| `h2-f64-active` | 8.99 GiB | 9.31 GiB | −3% |
| `h2-f32` | 14.82 GiB | 15.99 GiB | −7% |

Better than the −20…−25% calibrated from older records, so the default 1.25
headroom is now conservative. It is left as is: the model underestimates by
construction (library buffers only, no Python heap), and a headroom that
tracks measurement noise is worth more than a tighter one.

## Comparison with the pre-correction record

The comparable historical entry is the 2026-07-10 fresh-reboot run on the same
rfgen study surface (area 4.6e-4), standard path: **float 396 s / 25 it**,
double 1168 s / 38 it.

Against that, the corrected solver's standard float run takes **43 iterations
and 647 s** on the same surface and configuration. The extra iterations are the
price of the certified stopping rule: the old run stopped on Σp|g| alone, which
says nothing about penetration where the pressure is zero, while this one must
also drive the Frank–Wolfe certificate and the penetration below tolerance.
The plan anticipates exactly this — "an increased iteration count needed to
reject false convergence is not a regression in time to a correct solution."

Two caveats against over-reading it: the runs are from different sessions
months apart with unknown machine load, and each is a single repetition. The
iteration counts (25 → 43) are the more trustworthy half of the comparison,
since they are load-independent.

**Note that the active-set path more than absorbs the cost**: 124.7 s
certified is 3.2× faster than the 396 s the uncertified standard path took.

## Consequences for the D3 plan

The measured phase split reorders the optimisation packages:

* **A08 (displacement recurrence) and A10 (M2L compression) optimise the
  matvec**, which is 4–6% of an active-set run at this size. Driving it to
  zero would save 4–6%, below the 10% median improvement the promotion rule
  requires of a new default. Combined with the pre-existing negative evidence
  (tensor transfers 0.65×/0.61× at q=4/6; M2L flop ratio 1.175 at q=4;
  recurrence 18% of matvecs but 4% of wall time, precondition holding 8 of 22
  iterations), neither looks promotable on this workload.
* **A13 (sparse preconditioner with a coarse space)**, currently parked in D4
  research, targets 41–47%.
* **The coarse cascade — 45–50% — is in no package at all.** A uniform
  `coarse_tol=1e-4` across nine levels has never been examined, and it needs no
  new algorithm.

None of this refutes A08/A10 as *ideas*; it says they are aimed at the wrong
4%. A dilute-contact or smaller-Ns workload could weight the shares
differently, and B04 (candidate density sweep) is the measurement that would
show it.

## Reproduce

```bash
OMP_NUM_THREADS=20 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 setsid nohup \
  python bench/harness.py run --workload rough-H0.8 --ns 16384 \
    --variants h2-f32-active,h2-f64-active,h2-f32,h2-f64 \
  > data/bench_16384.log 2>&1 < /dev/null & disown
python bench/analyze.py --pair h2-f32,h2-f32-active --metric wall_cold_s
```

Must be launched **outside the agent sandbox** — see `bench/README.md`.

## Ledger caveat

The three completed rows were recorded at `982ef0c`, before the fix that makes
the active path report the caller's `requested_tol` rather than the clamped
level tolerance. Their `requested_tol` field therefore reads `2e-6` instead of
`1e-8` for `h2-f32-active`, and `analyze.py`'s accuracy gate will disqualify a
paired comparison against them until they are re-run. The measured physics is
unaffected — the bug was in one metadata field, not in the solve — and the
comparison above was made by hand with that in mind. A clean re-run at the
current revision would cost about 20 minutes.
